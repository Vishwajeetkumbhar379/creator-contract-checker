# Contract check · sample agreement

**Score:** 0/100 · **Verdict:** negotiate before signing

| Term | Found |
|---|---|
| paid amplification | yes |
| exclusivity | 6 months |
| payment terms | 90 days |

### 🔴 Usage rights never expire
*high · affects the creator*

The brand can use the content forever, which normally costs several times a standard fee.

> Creator grants Brand the irrevocable right to use, edit and repurpose the content in perpetuity across all media.

**Ask for:** Limit usage to 6-12 months, or price perpetual use as a separate line item.

### 🔴 Broad or long exclusivity without a fee
*high · affects the creator*

Blocking a whole category or several months can cost the creator other income.

> During the campaign and for six (6) months thereafter, Creator shall not promote any other skincare or beauty brand.

**Ask for:** Name the direct competitors, keep it to the campaign plus 30-90 days, and pay an exclusivity fee.

### 🔴 Payment in 90 days
*high · affects the creator*

Waiting more than two months strains a creator's cash flow.

> Creator shall invoice the Brand and payment shall be made within 90 days following the end of the campaign.

**Ask for:** Ask for net 30, or 50% upfront and 50% on posting.

### 🔴 Unlimited revisions
*high · affects the creator*

Open-ended revisions can turn one post into weeks of work.

> Creator shall make revisions as requested until the Brand is satisfied.

**Ask for:** Cap it at two rounds; extra rounds billed.

### 🔴 Creator gives up ownership
*high · affects the creator*

Assigning ownership is far more than a licence: the creator loses control of their own content.

> Creator hereby assigns all right, title and interest in the content to the Brand.

**Ask for:** Grant a limited licence instead and keep ownership.

### 🟠 Paid ads use with no extra fee
*medium · affects the creator*

Running the content as ads (whitelisting) is worth more than organic posting.

> Brand may use the content in paid social advertising, including whitelisting through Creator's account.

**Ask for:** Add a paid-usage fee and cap the ad flight duration.

### 🟠 Payment clock starts late
*medium · affects the creator*

Starting the clock at campaign end or brand approval can add weeks of delay.

> Creator shall invoice the Brand and payment shall be made within 90 days following the end of the campaign.

**Ask for:** Start the payment clock on the posting date or on invoice receipt.

### 🟠 Raw footage must be handed over
*medium · affects the creator*

Raw files let the brand cut new ads without paying for new content.

> Creator shall also deliver all raw footage to the Brand.

**Ask for:** Deliver final edits only, or charge for raw files.

### 🟠 No kill fee
*medium · affects the creator*

If the brand cancels late, the creator may not be paid for work already done.

> Brand may terminate this Agreement at any time with written notice.

**Ask for:** Add a cancellation fee, for example 50% after the brief is accepted and 100% after content is delivered.

### 🟠 Automatic renewal
*medium · affects the both*

The deal (and any exclusivity) can roll on without anyone deciding.

> This Agreement shall automatically renew for successive 3-month periods unless terminated.

**Ask for:** Require written agreement to renew.

### 🟠 No ad-disclosure clause
*medium · affects the both*

Both sides carry risk if a sponsored post isn't labelled. Rules differ by country (Germany expects 'Werbung' or 'Anzeige').

**Ask for:** Name the exact label and placement, and switch on the platform's paid-partnership tag.

### 🟡 No deadline for brand approval
*low · affects the both*

Slow approvals push posting dates and payment.

> All content is subject to the Brand's approval before posting.

**Ask for:** Brand gives feedback within 3 business days or the draft counts as approved.

### 🟡 One-sided morality clause
*low · affects the creator*

Only the brand can walk away over reputation issues.

> If Creator acts in a way that brings the Brand into disrepute, Brand may terminate immediately.

**Ask for:** Make it mutual, so the creator can also exit if the brand faces a scandal.

### 🟡 No governing law
*low · affects the both*

If something goes wrong, it's unclear which country's courts apply.

**Ask for:** Add a governing-law and jurisdiction clause.
