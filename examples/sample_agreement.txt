INFLUENCER MARKETING AGREEMENT (FICTIONAL SAMPLE FOR TESTING)

This Agreement is made between Nordlys Skin GmbH ("Brand") and Lena Glow ("Creator").

1. Services. Creator shall produce one Instagram Reel and three Stories promoting the Barrier Repair Serum. Creator shall make revisions as requested until the Brand is satisfied. All content is subject to the Brand's approval before posting. Creator shall also deliver all raw footage to the Brand.

2. Fee. In consideration of the Services, Brand shall pay Creator a fee of EUR 1,800. Creator shall invoice the Brand and payment shall be made within 90 days following the end of the campaign.

3. Usage rights. Creator grants Brand the irrevocable right to use, edit and repurpose the content in perpetuity across all media. Brand may use the content in paid social advertising, including whitelisting through Creator's account.

4. Exclusivity. During the campaign and for six (6) months thereafter, Creator shall not promote any other skincare or beauty brand.

5. Ownership. Creator hereby assigns all right, title and interest in the content to the Brand.

6. Termination. Brand may terminate this Agreement at any time with written notice. If Creator acts in a way that brings the Brand into disrepute, Brand may terminate immediately.

7. Term. This Agreement shall automatically renew for successive 3-month periods unless terminated.
