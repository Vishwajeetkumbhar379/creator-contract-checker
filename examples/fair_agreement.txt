FAIR CREATOR AGREEMENT (FICTIONAL SAMPLE FOR TESTING)

1. Services. Creator will produce one Instagram Reel. The fee includes two rounds of revisions; further rounds are billed at EUR 150 each. Brand will give feedback within 3 business days of receiving a draft.

2. Fee and payment. Brand will pay a fee of EUR 1,800 net 30 from receipt of invoice. Creator may invoice on the posting date. Late payments carry statutory late payment interest.

3. Usage. Creator grants Brand a licence to use the content on Brand's organic social channels for 12 months in Germany, Austria and Switzerland. Paid advertising use requires a separate usage fee of EUR 600 per month.

4. Exclusivity. For 30 days after posting, Creator will not promote the named competitors listed in Annex A, in consideration of the exclusivity fee of EUR 400.

5. Disclosure. Creator will label each post with "Werbung" at the start of the caption and use the platform's paid partnership tag.

6. Cancellation. If Brand cancels after the brief is accepted, Brand pays a kill fee of 50% of the fee. Either party may end the agreement if the other party brings it into disrepute.

7. Governing law. This agreement is governed by the laws of Germany.
